BACKGROUND:
Our team is developing OligoTox, a mechanism-aware toxicity prediction platform
for RNA and oligonucleotide therapeutics. The platform integrates sequence features,
chemistry descriptors (PS fraction, LNA pattern, sugar modification), delivery
modality (GalNAc, LNP, naked), and multi-omics readouts to generate endpoint-specific
safety risk scores across 13 organ-level toxicity endpoints.

OligoTox is specifically designed to address the gap you identified in the challenge:
the lack of open, high-quality, structured datasets for training and benchmarking
in silico oligo toxicity predictors.

OUR DATA GENERATION PROPOSAL:
We propose to generate and contribute:

1. Extended LNA gapmer hepatotoxicity dataset
   - 200+ additional gapmers beyond Papargyri 2020 Table S1
   - Readouts: caspase-3/7 activation, LDH, ATP viability (human HepG2 + HepaRG)
   - Chemistry variants: LNA content (10-60%), flank asymmetry, PS fraction

2. PS/LNA gapmer innate immune activation panel
   - 50+ gapmers tested in human PBMCs
   - Readouts: TLR7/8/9 reporter, IFN-alpha, IL-6, TNF-alpha
   - Links TLR activation to sequence motifs (UU/GU, CpG) and PS density

3. GalNAc siRNA complement activation panel
   - 20+ GalNAc siRNA variants with formulation metadata
   - Readouts: C3a, C5a, SC5b-9, platelet-bound C3d/C4d in human serum
   - Bridges our current Domain 3 rules engine to quantitative in vitro data

PLATFORM ALIGNMENT:
Our existing platform infrastructure (rules engine, feature engineering layer,
ML scaffold, assay planner) is already built and ready to incorporate your
datasets as training and validation data once released.

We believe OligoTox is well-positioned to serve as a prediction platform that
directly demonstrates the utility of the OligoTox Challenge datasets for Phase 2.
